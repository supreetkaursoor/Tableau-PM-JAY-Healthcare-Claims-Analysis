🏥 Healthcare Insurance Claims & Fraud Analytics Dashboard
#📌 Project Overview

An interactive healthcare analytics project designed to analyze insurance claims, hospital performance, fraudulent activities, and disease-wise claim patterns through four dashboard modules.

#📈 Dashboard Modules
Executive Summary: Claim status, financial overview, monthly trends, and state-wise analysis.
Hospital Performance: Hospital claim amounts, approval rates, and government vs. private comparison.
Fraud Analytics: Monthly fraud trends, state-wise fraud, hospital comparison, and package-wise fraud.
Disease & Package Analytics: Top diseases, specialty-wise claims, monthly trends, and treatment package costs.

#🔍 Key Business Insights
West Bengal recorded the highest state-wise claim amount at approximately ₹275.02 million.
Hospital 581 recorded the highest hospital claim amount of ₹9.53 million.
Hospital 453 achieved the highest approval rate of 53.66%.
March recorded the highest monthly fraud count with 513 cases.
West Bengal reported the highest state-wise fraud count of 533.
Appendectomy recorded the highest disease claim count (8,443) and package cost (₹152.61 million).
